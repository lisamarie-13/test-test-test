Our Story

Built by accident

Glitchcraft started when a bartender, a chemist, and a DJ walked into a garage in East Austin. That's not a joke — that's literally how this started. We wanted to make drinks that didn't exist yet.

Rules

No dress code. No pretension. No rules about what a margarita should be.

We believe the margarita is the most underestimated cocktail in existence. Most bars treat it as an afterthought — sour mix, cheap tequila, salt, done. We treat it as a canvas. Every ingredient is intentional. Every technique is borrowed from somewhere unexpected. We ferment our own syrups. We clarify juice with milk. We smoke things that probably shouldn't be smoked. The result is something familiar but completely new.
